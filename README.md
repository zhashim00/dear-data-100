# dear-data-100

Dear Data 100: a record of my training days.

One row per day, including rest days.

## Columns

| Column       | Meaning                                                          |
|--------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| date         | yyyy-mm-dd                                                       |
| day_type     | arms, chest_back, legs or rest (what my plan said for that day)  |
| activity     | what I actually did: [list your values, e.g. lifting, running]   |
| duration_min | length of the session in whole minutes (number only)             |
| effort       | 1 to 10, where [define 1 and 10 in your own words]               |

## Data source

[One or two honest sentences: where the numbers come from (for
example, my Hevy workout log), when I started recording, and
whether any rows were entered after the day they happened.]

## Notes

[Anything a reader needs to interpret the data, e.g. what counts as
a rest day, or how I handled a missed day.]
